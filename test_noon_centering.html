Noon Centering Verification Test

Test Purpose

This page tests whether noon (12 PM) appears in the visual center of the 24-hour temperature chart.

A red line shows the exact visual center of the chart area. If noon centering is working correctly, the noon marker (🌞 12 PM) should be very close to this red center line.

Current Implementation Test

Chart with axis range: -0.5 to 23.5 (should center noon)

Visual Center

Analysis:

  • The red line marks the exact visual center of the chart
  • With range -0.5 to 23.5 (24 units total), the mathematical center is at position 11.5
  • Noon is at position 12, which is only 0.5 units from center
  • This should make noon appear very close to the visual center

Original Implementation (for comparison)

Chart with axis range: 0 to 23 (original - noon NOT centered)

Visual Center

Analysis:

  • With range 0 to 23 (24 units total), the mathematical center is at position 11.5
  • Noon is at position 12, which is 0.5 units from center
  • But the visual effect is that noon appears slightly right of center

Expected Results

First chart (improved): The 🌞 12 PM marker should be very close to the red center line.

Second chart (original): The 🌞 12 PM marker should appear slightly to the right of the red center line.
